# Thesis structure, front matter, page numbering

## 1. Overall order and primary components
**RULE**, printed p. 6 (PDF p. 8): a thesis contains (a) introductory/front matter, (b) main text, (c) references, (d) appendices, (e) researcher's biography. The exact collection depends on content and medium; do not omit a required item silently.

### Covers and approval
- Outer cover: approved hardbound thesis; cover color is **deep orange** matching university color, gold lettering in a conventional font, cover font size about **16–24 pt**, chosen for fit (printed p. 6; PDF 8). This physical-cover rule is **not** automatically required for ordinary class reports or digital-only assignments.
- Inner title / approval sheet: thesis title (Thai or English according to thesis language); author full name with proper title; degree's approved abbreviated full description/program and specialization; academic position of thesis supervisor (printed pp. 6–7; PDF 8–9).
- **EXAMPLES**: spine printed p. 25 (PDF 27); Thai inner cover printed p. 26 (PDF 28); English title printed p. 27 (PDF 29); Thai thesis-approval printed p. 28 (PDF 30); English approval printed p. 29 (PDF 31).
- Do not copy illustrative author or committee names from the manual. Obtain institution-approved degree and department wording before finalizing.

### Preliminary pages
**RULE**, printed pp. 7–8 (PDF 9–10):
1. Abstract Thai, then English if the thesis is written in Thai; all thesis languages require both Thai and English abstracts (or the institution's current applicable equivalent). Examples printed pp. 30–31 (PDF 32–33).
2. Acknowledgments / กิตติกรรมประกาศ — formal and concise, generally **no more than 1 page**; printed p. 7, example p. 32 (PDF 34).
3. Table of contents / สารบัญ — example pp. 33–34 (PDF 35–36).
4. List of tables / รายการตาราง — including appendices, example pp. 35–36 (PDF 37–38).
5. List of figures / รายการรูปประกอบ — including appendices, example p. 37 (PDF 39).
6. Symbols / รายการสัญลักษณ์ where symbols are used — example p. 38 (PDF 40).
7. Technical vocabulary and abbreviations / ประมวลศัพท์เทคนิคและประมวลคำย่อ where used — example p. 39 (PDF 41).

**RULE, abstract**: Abstract briefly covers objectives, hypotheses if any, research methods, results and conclusions; not extra interpretation. Thai abstract comes before English in a Thai-language thesis; each abstract should be **no more than one page**. Keywords **3–8**, separated by `/` (printed p. 7; PDF 9). English abstract is a proper translation of Thai, not a new unrelated abstract.

### Main body
**RULE**, printed pp. 8–9 (PDF 10–11): content can be in Thai or English. For Thai spelling of foreign words, follow the cited transliteration/standard usage; chapters generally cover:
1. Introduction / บทนำ
2. Related theories and research / ทฤษฎี/งานวิจัยที่เกี่ยวข้อง
3. Experimental or research methods / วิธีการทดลอง/ระเบียบวิธีวิจัย
4. Results / ผลการทดลอง/วิจัย
5. Conclusions, discussion and recommendations / สรุป/อภิปรายผล/ข้อเสนอแนะ

These are thematic requirements/illustrations — adapt headings to actual discipline and project, and do not invent experiments.

### Back matter
- References / เอกสารอ้างอิง required and placed after main body, before appendices; complete metadata for works actually cited. Printed p. 9 (PDF 11), bibliography formatting in pp. 14–23 (PDF 16–25).
- Appendices / ภาคผนวก: detailed supplementary analysis/data, instrument drawings/programs, long derivations not fit for the main narrative. Printed p. 9 (PDF 11).
- Researcher biography / ประวัติผู้วิจัย: **no more than one page**; name, birth date, education and institutions/years, research grant (if any), published articles and other applicable achievements (printed pp. 9–10, PDF 11–12); example printed p. 43, PDF 45.

## 2. Ordering and page numbering
**RULE**, printed p. 12 (PDF 14):
- Cover and approval sheet: **no printed page number**.
- Thai-language **front matter**: Thai consonant sequence **ก, ข, ค, ...** (not Arabic numerals); exact starting point depends on which first numbered prelim is present.
- English-language **front matter**: lowercase Roman **i, ii, iii, ...**. The manual shows examples with lowercase Roman, not uppercase.
- Main content (chapters, references, appendices, biography): Arabic numerals **1, 2, 3, ...**, printed **at the upper-right**.
- First page of every chapter: page number **not displayed**, but the page is **counted**; visible numbering resumes continuously on following page.
- **Unclear in source**: whether all appendix heading pages must suppress visible numbers as chapters do. If an appendix is structured as a chapter and user wants same rule, document the choice.

**IMPLEMENTATION for DOCX**
1. Use separate Word sections for title/approval, preliminary pages, and main numbered text. Use an additional section per chapter only if needed for first-page suppression; use `different_first_page_header_footer` cautiously.
2. Avoid resetting main Arabic numbering at each chapter. Only the first main chapter should start at 1.
3. Use dedicated PAGE fields or generated sequential page labels (Thai ก ข ค may require a custom field or individual sections); do not simply substitute roman numbering in a Thai thesis.
4. Manually check the entire rendered PDF's upper-right and chapter first pages. Verify TOC after all page numbers are final.
5. No fixed header/page-number X–Y offset is given; place upper-right within the usable header and maintain consistent typography.

## 3. Tables of contents and front-page specimens
- Table of contents (printed pp. 33–34 / PDF 35–36) shows front matter entries and Arabic chapter subsections; use **real headings** and actual page references.
- Lists of tables (printed pp. 35–36 / PDF 37–38) and figures (printed p. 37 / PDF 39) include captions and page numbers, and a continuation header when needed.
- Symbols, abbreviations and glossary (printed pp. 38–39 / PDF 40–41) have separate structures; include only when relevant, except where your department requires them.
- Acknowledgments example (printed p. 32 / PDF 34) and abstract examples (printed pp. 30–31 / PDF 32–33) illustrate hierarchy and spacing; don't reuse actual example text as submission content.

- Printed pp. 13 and 44–52 (PDF pp. 15 and 46–54) include **2013-era** binding, library copies, copyright agreements, committee processes and electronic submission procedures, references to CD-R, software and file naming. Treat them as historical, **do not assert they are current in 2026**.
